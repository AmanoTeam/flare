# flare

A command-line utility to crop white and black gutters off images.

## Why

Gutters are long empty spaces that appear between panels in webtoons. They're usually used to separate scenes or create transitions, but some webtoons abuse them to the point where reading becomes a pain. They take up too much screen space and force you to do many more swipes just to scroll past them.
